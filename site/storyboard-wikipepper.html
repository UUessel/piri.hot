2 · Log a burnToday

Pick the pepper, whole or in a dish, how much, the pain with five little Piri's, the taste, a private note. The app does the Scoville arithmetic.

3 · The Peppers tabToday

The catalog: search, heat filters, one row per pepper. "Which pepper is this?" looks a pepper up by colour, shape and size. Today: 315 peppers, 107 switched on, from Wikidata, Wikipedia, books and a university publication.

4 · The pepper pageToday

Photo with heat stage and credit, names, your burns, heat with "how many jalapeños", origin, story, fun fact from Piri, facts, names in other languages, the "not yet checked" note, "I ate this". Stays exactly like this.

Part 2How WikiPepper fits in · WikiPepper becomes the one source of the catalog, says so on every screen, and the app sends people to WikiPepper.

12 · Both waysProposal

WikiPepper stays the single source of truth: its changes come in every night through an API, an automatic exchange between the two systems. Corrections go back: a user taps "Tell WikiPepper", Wessel checks it once, the curators get it.
